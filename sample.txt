The district court granted summary judgment. Under Monell v. Department of Social Services, 436 U.S. 658, 690-91 (1978), a municipality is liable only where an official policy causes the injury. Accord Bd. of Cnty. Comm'rs v. Brown, 520 U.S. 397, 403 (1997). The Tenth Circuit reaffirmed this in Schneider v. City of Grand Junction, 717 F.3d 760, 770 (10th Cir. 2013), and again in Waller v. City & County of Denver, 932 F.3d 1277, 1284 (10th Cir. 2019). Id. at 1285. Excessive force claims arise under Graham v. Connor, 490 U.S. 386, 395 (1989). Brown, supra, at 404.
